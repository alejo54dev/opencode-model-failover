### Failover fix for retryable errors
- **Problem**: Rate limits (429, 5xx) were classified as `"retry"` in `session.error` but the handler only triggered failover on `"immediate"`. Plugin relied on `session.status` events for retries, but OpenCode may not emit them consistently for rate limits → red static message, no failover.
- **Fix**: `session.error` handler now triggers failover on BOTH `"immediate"` AND `"retry"` actions — `src/index.ts:294-303`
- The `executeFailover` guards (cooldown, failoverInProgress) prevent spam.
